Ask on WhatsApp
Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first consultation — in person, on phone or on video call — is completely free and carries no obligation to buy anything.

What does “Lifetime FREE Service” include?

Annual policy reviews, claim assistance, nominee/address/mobile updates, premium reminders, portfolio reviews, insurance need analysis and general financial consultations — free for as long as you are my client.

Will you help my family during a claim?

Absolutely. I personally guide your family through documentation, submission and follow-up with the company until the claim is settled. This is the most important part of my job.

Can you review policies I bought from someone else?

Yes. I'm happy to review any existing policy or mutual fund portfolio and give you an honest opinion on whether it suits your needs.

How much do I need to start a SIP?

You can start a mutual fund SIP with as little as ₹500 per month in many schemes. We'll decide the right amount based on your goals and budget.

Do you offer home visits or online meetings?

Yes. You can visit my office in Vasai West, or I can meet you at your home, or we can connect over phone, WhatsApp or video call — whatever is convenient for you.

What documents should I bring for the first meeting?

Just bring copies of your existing policies or investment statements, if any. For new plans we'll need PAN, Aadhaar, photographs and bank details — I'll guide you step by step.

Is my personal information safe with you?

Yes. Your information is used only to advise and service you, and is never sold or shared with anyone without your consent. See our Privacy Policy for details.
